01Information that stays on your device

Expenses, income, budgets, investments, savings goals, credit cards, loans, reminders, and any receipt photos or PDFs you attach are stored on your phone. They are not sent to us, whether or not you sign in.

Renewvio is published by Achyut Raghuvanshi (“we”, “us”).

02Information we store when you sign in

Signing in is optional and uses your Google account. When you sign in, we store the following:

InformationWhy we store it
Name, email address and Google account identifierTo create and identify your account
Subscriptions: service, amount, currency, billing cycle, renewal date, statusTo back them up and sync them across your devices
Shared household data: name, members, shared expenses, splits, repaymentsTo show them to the members of that household
A push address for each phone you are signed in on (a token issued by Google)To notify you about a shared household while the app is closed. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account.
Usage events, such as a feature being opened (counts only, no names or amounts)To learn which features are used and to fix problems

This information is held by our hosting provider, Supabase, and always travels over an encrypted connection.

03Advertising

Renewvio is free because it shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To deliver and measure those ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • which ads you see and tap, and diagnostic information.

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change that choice at any time under SettingsPrivacy & ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. Google explains how it uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertisers never receive the subscriptions, expenses or other financial information you enter in Renewvio.

05Permissions

  • Notifications and exact alarms, to remind you about renewals and bills at the time you chose.
  • Internet, for sign-in, sync, logos and ads.
  • Advertising ID, used by Google AdMob as described in section 03.

Renewvio does not read your SMS messages, contacts or call log. A receipt is read only when you share it into the app yourself, and it is processed on your phone.

06Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account and everything stored with it, and erases Renewvio's data from the phone you delete from. Records you added to a shared household stay in that household's ledger for its other members, shown as “Former member” without your name.

Data that only ever lived on your phone is removed when you erase the app's data or uninstall it.

How to delete your account

07Your choices

  • Use Renewvio without signing in, so nothing leaves your phone except what advertising needs.
  • Turn personalised ads off in Settings.
  • Ask us what account data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by email.
  • Delete your account and its data at any time.

08Children

Renewvio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

09Changes to this policy
