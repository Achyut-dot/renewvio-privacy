Privacy policy

Your money is personal. So is your data.

This page explains, in plain language, what Renewvio keeps on your device, what it stores when you sign in, and how advertising works in the app.

Last updated 22 November 2026 4 minute read

Works without an account

Use Renewvio signed out and everything you enter stays on your device.

Sign-in is your choice

Sign in with Google only if you want backup, sync or a shared household.

Free, with ads

Banner ads from Google AdMob keep Renewvio free. Nothing in the app is sold.

Delete it any time

Remove your account and its data from Settings in a few taps.

01

Information stored only on your device

Expenses, income, budgets, investments, savings goals, credit cards, loans, reminders and any receipt photos or PDFs you attach are stored on your device. They are not sent to us.

Renewvio is published by Achyut Raghuvanshi. We do not sell your personal information.
02

Information we collect when you sign in

Signing in is optional and uses your Google account. When you sign in we store:

Information
Why
Your name, email address and Google account identifier
To create and identify your account
Your subscriptions: service name, amount, currency, billing cycle, renewal date and status
To back them up and sync them between your devices
Shared household data: household name, members, shared expenses, splits and repayments
To show them to the members of that household
Usage events, such as a feature being used, with counts but no names or amounts
To understand which features are used and to fix problems

This data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and is sent over an encrypted connection.

03

Advertising

Renewvio shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic information.

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice from the app's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings. Google describes its use of this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not give advertisers the subscriptions, expenses or other financial information you enter in the app.
04

Other services we use

  • Google Sign-In is used only if you choose to sign in.
  • Supabase stores the account data described in section 02.
  • Logo providers supply public service logos, for example for Netflix or Spotify. The request contains the service's name or domain, not your personal data.
05

Permissions

  • Notifications and exact alarms, to remind you about renewals and bills at the time you chose.
  • Internet, for sign-in, sync, logos and ads.
  • Advertising ID, used by Google AdMob as described above.

Renewvio does not read your SMS messages, contacts or call log. A receipt is processed only when you share it into the app yourself, and it is processed on your device.

06

Keeping and deleting your data

Account data is kept until you delete your account. You can delete your account and its data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account. Data stored only on your device is removed when you erase the app's data or uninstall the app.
