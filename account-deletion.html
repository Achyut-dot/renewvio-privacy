Delete your Renewvio account

You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app, in under a minute. Deletion happens immediately.

  • Takes effect immediately
  • Cannot be undone
  • Email option if you can't open the app

01Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Settings

    On the Home screen, tap your profile picture in the top-left corner.

  2. Go to Danger zone

    Scroll to the bottom of Settings and tap Delete account under Danger zone. You see this section only while you are signed in.

  3. Confirm

    Read the summary in Permanently delete account? and tap Delete permanently. Renewvio deletes your account and returns to its welcome screens.

Own a shared household? If other people are still in it, hand it over or delete it first under MoneySharedMembers. The app tells you if this applies.

This cannot be undone. Deleted data cannot be recovered, by you or by us.

03If you use Renewvio on another phone

Expenses, income, budgets, investments and goals are never stored online, so deleting your account cannot reach a second phone. Open Renewvio there and tap Sign out and erase data in Settings, or clear the app's storage in Android settings.

04If you can't open the app

Email us from the address you sign in with, so we can match the request to your account, and ask us to delete it.
