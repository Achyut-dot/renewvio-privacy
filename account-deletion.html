Account deletion

Delete your Renewvio account

You can delete your Renewvio account, and everything stored with it, from inside the app. It takes a few taps and completes immediately.

Settings → Account → Delete account

Open Settings

In Renewvio, tap your profile picture on the Home screen to open Settings.

Choose Delete account

Go to Account and tap Delete account.

Confirm

Tap Delete permanently. Your account is removed straight away.

Deletion is permanent. It completes immediately and cannot be undone.

Deleted from the cloud

Your authentication account and everything Renewvio stores for you online:

  • your profile
  • synced subscriptions
  • usage records
  • parser records

Erased from this device

All Renewvio data on the device you delete from:

  • subscriptions, expenses and bills
  • income and budgets
  • investments and goals
  • scheduled reminders

If you use a second device

Expenses, income, budgets, investments and goals are only ever stored on your device. If you use Renewvio on a second device, open the app there and choose Sign out and erase data, or clear the app's storage in Android settings, to remove that copy too.

Can't open the app?

Email us from the address associated with your account and ask for your account to be deleted. No data is retained after deletion.
