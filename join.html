How to join

  1. Have Renewvio already?

    Tap Open in Renewvio. The app opens with the code filled in.

  2. New to Renewvio?

    Get it free from Google Play, sign in with Google, then come back to this page and tap Open in Renewvio.

  3. If the button does nothing

    Tap Copy code. In Renewvio, tap the people icon on Home, then Join with a code, then Paste.

The code works once and expires seven days after it was made. It was not sent to this website: it stays in the address on your phone. Other members see only what is added to the shared household, never your own expenses, income or subscriptions. Privacy policy
